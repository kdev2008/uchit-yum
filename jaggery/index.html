The Difference

Why choose Natural Jaggery?

Refined white sugar strips away everything — minerals, molasses, character. Jaggery keeps it all intact. Slow-heated in iron pans, it retains iron, magnesium, potassium and natural antioxidants that processing destroys.

Uchit Yum Jaggery Powder is sourced directly from Indian farms — no intermediaries, no sulphur treatment, no bleaching agents. Just pure, honest sweetness exactly as nature intended.
