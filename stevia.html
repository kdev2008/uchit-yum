The Plant

What is Stevia?

Stevia is a small plant native to South America whose leaves are naturally 300 times sweeter than sugar — with zero calories and zero glycemic impact. It has been used as a natural sweetener for centuries.

Uchit Yum Stevia is extracted directly from the leaf, with no artificial processing. The result: pure, clean sweetness you can trust — in your tea, your coffee, your baking, your whole life.
